Why Early Support Matters

Early Treatment is Most Effective

For a young person with symptoms of a mental disorder, the earlier treatment is started, the more effective it can be — helping prevent more severe, lasting problems as a child grows up.

Most Symptoms Begin Before Age 18

Youth is a critical period for mental health. Most people living with a mental illness first experience symptoms before they turn 18.

Only 1 in 5 Children Get Help

In Canada, only 1 out of 5 children and adolescents receive appropriate mental health services — leaving too many without the support they need.

Recognizing the Signs

Depressed children and youth often lose interest in activities they once enjoyed. Anxious children may feel frightened, anticipate the worst, and expect bad things to happen.
